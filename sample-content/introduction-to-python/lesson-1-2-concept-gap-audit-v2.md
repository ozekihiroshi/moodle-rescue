## Quality gates

- The learner predicts, runs, changes, and explains at least one state change.
- Every mandatory concept appears in the lesson, Notebook, and learning check.
- Common mistakes are executable or traceable, not merely named.
- The English source and Japanese adaptation have equivalent evidence.
- The learning check contains ten misconception-driven questions.
- Teacher notes give diagnostic prompts and a short transfer check.
